Websites for contractors and local trades

A contractor's website has one job: help a customer on a phone reach you fast. We build new sites and rebuild old ones for contractors, trades and local service businesses, using your real photos and facts.

What you get

  1. A phone-first site. Tap-to-call, plus a call or quote button on the first screen of a phone, so nobody has to scroll to find you.
  2. A quote form that reaches you. Every request lands in your inbox and on a call list. We send a test request before launch to prove it works.
  3. Your real photos and facts. If we don't know your license number or your warranty terms, we ask. We never make them up.
  4. A page for each main service. Area pages only go up where there's something real to say about that town, not copy-paste city pages.
  5. Search basics built in. Every page gets a clear title and description and one main headline, and the site gets a sitemap, a robots.txt file and business schema.
  6. Ready for AI search tools. The site has structured data, an answer-style FAQ and an llms.txt file. See AI search visibility.
  7. Light on phones. Compressed images and as few scripts as possible.
  8. Checked before you see it. A script checks every page for made-up facts, banned words and broken links, and then a quality check reviews it.

How a build goes

  1. Free audit or a quick call. Have a site? The free audit checks it. No site? Leave your number.
  2. Nathan calls you. He asks what work you want more of, where you work and what the site needs to do. Then he gives you a straight quote.
  3. You send photos and facts. Phone photos of real jobs are fine. We ask for anything that's missing.
  4. We build and you check. Nothing goes live without your yes.
  5. After launch. Hosting + Care keeps it running, if you want it.

Rebuild, or keep what you have?

If your current site lets a customer on a phone find your number and send a request in a few seconds, keep it. The audit shows where it falls short, and the fix list is yours whoever does the work. See a sample audit.

You own it

Your domain is in your name from day one, and the site is yours once the setup is paid.

What we won't do

  1. Promise rankings. Nobody honestly can.
  2. Pass off stock photos as your work, or write reviews you didn't get.
  3. Bury a website fee inside some other bill. You see what each part covers.

Questions

Do I own the website?

Yes. Your domain is in your name from day one, and the site is yours once the setup is paid.

Do I need professional photos?

No. Clear phone photos of real jobs work. We'd rather show your real work than stock pictures.

Will it show up on Google?

We build every page so search engines can read it: titles, headings, a sitemap and business schema. We never promise rankings. Nobody honestly can. Local SEO is the ongoing work.

Can you keep my current domain?

Yes, if it's registered in your name. If a past vendor holds it, Nathan can walk you through asking for it back.
