Stop U.S. inquiries from getting lost

Small automations that collect your inquiries in one place, send an English reply straight away, and remind your team to follow up, so buyers aren't left waiting across time zones.

Common problems for U.S. sales

  • Inquiries land in one person's inbox and get missed
  • Buyers wait a day or more because of the time difference
  • Quotes are retyped from scratch every time
  • Nobody knows which buyers are waiting for a follow-up

  1. 1

What you get

Mini workflows and automation

  • Instant inquiry alerts by email
  • Automatic English acknowledgment to the buyer
  • One shared inquiry list or simple CRM
  • Follow-up reminders and a stale-lead list
  • Quote and sample-request templates
  • A one-page guide for your team

Signs you need this

  • You've lost track of buyers who wrote to you
  • Replies take more than a U.S. business day
  • Quotes are rebuilt from scratch each time
  • Only one person knows the status of each inquiry
  • You copy inquiries into spreadsheets by hand

Questions about this service

Which tools do you use?

The simplest reliable option for your team, often tools you already use. We avoid adding paid software unless it clearly pays for itself, and we tell you any cost up front.

Will automatic replies feel robotic?

They're short, clear and honest: they confirm we received the request and when a person will reply. Your team still writes the real answer.

Can it work with WhatsApp?

We can include WhatsApp as a channel your team uses to follow up, within WhatsApp's rules. We don't send bulk messages.
