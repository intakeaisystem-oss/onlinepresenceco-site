Questions about this service

Can you guarantee a ranking or an AI mention?

No. Nobody honestly can. We remove the reasons search engines and AI tools can't understand you, and we show you what changes.

What is llms.txt?

A proposed format: a plain text file that summarizes what your site is about and which pages matter. Some AI tools may read it; none are required to. It's quick to add and easy to keep current.

Do you build links or buy reviews?

No. We don't buy links, reviews or followers. We focus on clear, accurate pages and consistent facts.
