What we do for local businesses

We build your website, set up your Google listing and keep both working, so customers can find you and call you in one tap. Your audit or a short call decides which parts you need.

Book a callOr call Nathan: (630) 765-0559

The five services

  1. Websites. A new site or a rebuild, made for phones and built from your real photos and facts. Tap-to-call on every page and a quote form that reaches you.
  2. Local SEO and Google Business Profile. Your Google Business Profile set up and kept current. Your services and towns spelled out on your site. Your name, address and phone the same everywhere. A simple way to ask every customer for a review.
  3. AI search setup. Some people now ask ChatGPT, Google's AI Overviews or Perplexity who to call. We set up your site so those tools can read it and tell what you do and where. Each month we ask them your customers' real questions and show you what they said. We report what the AI tools say. We never promise placement.
  4. Visitor Insights. We watch your site's analytics and find where people leave and why. Each month you get a Leak Report: the top 3 places visitors drop off, the likely reason, the proof, and the fix.
  5. Hosting + Care. Hosting, the security certificate, backups, updates, uptime checks and small edits, plus your report every month.
