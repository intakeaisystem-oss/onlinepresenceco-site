Services

Everything we do has one goal: make it easier for an American buyer to understand you, trust you and send you a request. Start with one fix or ask for a full build.

English websites for U.S. buyersSEO and GEO (AI search)AI tools for your businessU.S. shipping and pricing fixesMini workflows and automationStore and checkout fixes

English websites for U.S. buyers

A fast, mobile-friendly company website written in clear American English: what you make, your capabilities, quality, certifications, MOQ and lead times, and a quote form that asks for the right details.

  • New site or a rebuild of the one you have
  • Product and capability pages buyers can scan
  • Hosting, security certificate, backups and updates
  • Your domain stays in your name

SEO and GEO (AI search)

Help Google and AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews understand what you make, so you can show up when U.S. buyers search.

  • Pages built around what buyers actually search
  • Structured data (schema) and an llms.txt file (an emerging standard)
  • Clear answers to buyer questions on every key page
  • Google Search Console setup and monthly checks

AI tools for your business

Small AI tools that use your own catalog and documents to draft answers faster. AI can make mistakes, so a person on your side reviews and stays in control.

  • An English product-question assistant for your site, with hand-off to your sales team
  • A quote-request assistant that collects drawings, quantities and deadlines
  • Catalog-to-English product page drafts for you to review
  • Inquiry translation so your team can read and reply quickly

U.S. shipping and pricing fixes

Show cost and delivery the way American buyers expect, so they can estimate the real price before they write.

  • Inches and pounds next to cm and kg
  • Billable (dimensional) weight and carton calculators on your site
  • Incoterms (EXW, FOB, DDP) explained in plain English
  • Delivery-time and landed-cost estimate forms you control

Mini workflows and automation

Small automations that stop inquiries from getting lost and cut manual copying and retyping.

  • Every inquiry to email and a shared sheet or CRM, instantly
  • An automatic English reply so the buyer knows you received it
  • Follow-up reminders and quote templates
  • Sample-request and reorder flows

Store and checkout fixes

If you sell directly to U.S. customers on your own store, we fix what makes them leave before paying.

  • U.S. shipping zones, rates and address formats
  • Size charts and specs in U.S. units
  • Checkout, trust and policy pages that answer common questions
  • Speed and mobile fixes

Or just one small fix

Many jobs are small: a quote form that finally works, a shipping calculator, rewriting your top 10 product pages in clear English, an automatic reply to new inquiries. Tell us the problem and we'll quote the fix.

How we quote

There is no price list. After a short conversation we send a written quote for the exact work, with what's included and how long it takes. Monthly hosting and care, if you want it, is quoted the same way.

What we don't do

  • Move freight or clear customs. We are not a freight forwarder or customs broker.
  • Give legal or tariff advice. We can link to official sources and to a licensed professional's answer on your site.
  • Buy fake reviews, followers or links, or promise rankings.
