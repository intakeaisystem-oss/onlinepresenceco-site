AI tools that help your team handle U.S. buyers

Small, practical AI tools built on your own catalog and documents. They draft and sort; a person on your team checks and decides.

Get a free gap check →What you get

Common problems for U.S. sales

  • The same buyer questions answered by hand every day
  • Inquiries in English that take time to read and reply to
  • Hundreds of products with no clear English descriptions
  • Quote requests missing drawings, quantities or deadlines

How we do it

  1. 1

    Pick one job

    We start with the task that eats the most time, such as answering product questions or drafting descriptions.

  2. 2

    Load your facts

    Your catalog, specs and policies become the source the tool is set up to answer from.

  3. 3

    Build with a hand-off

    The tool drafts or collects; anything uncertain goes straight to a person on your team.

  4. 4

    Test and review

    We test it on real questions with you before anything reaches a buyer, and keep improving it.

What you get

AI tools for your business

  • An English product-question assistant for your site
  • A quote-request assistant that collects drawings, quantities and dates
  • Draft English product pages from your catalog for review
  • Inquiry translation and reply drafts for your team
  • Clear limits: what the tool may and may not answer
  • A simple way to update the facts it uses

Signs you need this

  • Your sales team answers the same questions over and over
  • Reading and replying in English slows your team down
  • Your catalog is too big to rewrite by hand
  • Buyers write in outside your working hours
  • Quote requests arrive without the details you need

Questions about this service

Will the AI make things up?

AI can make mistakes. That's why we set the tool up to answer only from your own documents, to say when it doesn't know, and to pass uncertain questions to a person. We test this with you before any buyer sees it.

Does it replace our sales team?

No. It removes repetitive work so your team spends more time on serious buyers.

What does it run on?

We choose the simplest reliable setup for your case and explain the running costs in your quote before you decide.
