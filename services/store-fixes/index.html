Fix what stops U.S. customers at checkout

If you sell directly to American customers on your own store, small problems at checkout can cost sales. We look for them and, with your approval, fix what we find.

Get a free gap check →What you get

Common problems for U.S. sales

  • Shipping zones and rates that don't cover U.S. addresses properly
  • Sizes and specs in metric only
  • Missing or unclear return, shipping and contact pages
  • Slow pages and a checkout that's hard to use on a phone

How we do it

  1. 1

    Walk the checkout

    We walk through your store the way a U.S. customer would, on phone and desktop, up to the payment step.

  2. 2

    List the leaks

    Every step where a customer could hesitate or leave, ranked by how likely it is to stop a buyer.

  3. 3

    Fix in order

    Shipping, units, policies, trust details, speed: starting with the biggest leak.

  4. 4

    Check again

    We test the full checkout again after each fix.

What you get

Store and checkout fixes

  • U.S. shipping zones, rates and address formats
  • Size charts and specs in U.S. units
  • Clear shipping, returns and contact pages
  • Real trust details customers look for before paying: business name, contact details and clear policies
  • Speed and mobile layout fixes
  • A short report of what changed and why

Signs you need this

  • Customers add to cart but don't finish checkout
  • You get questions about shipping time and returns
  • Your size charts are metric only
  • Your store is slow on a phone
  • Your policy pages are missing or copied from a template

Questions about this service

Which store platforms do you work with?

Common ones such as Shopify and WooCommerce. Tell us what you use and we'll say honestly whether we can help.

Do you handle U.S. sales tax?

No. We can set up your platform's tax settings the way your accountant or tax advisor tells us to.

Can you fix one problem only?

Yes. We can start with the single biggest problem, for example unclear shipping information.
