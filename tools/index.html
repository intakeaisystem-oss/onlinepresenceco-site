Export gap check

Answer honestly. Each "no" is a gap a U.S. buyer may notice.

  1. You have your own company website (not only a marketplace store).

  2. Your website is written in clear, natural English.

  3. It loads fast on a phone in the U.S. and shows the secure HTTPS lock.

  4. Each product page lists sizes and weights in inches and pounds too.

  5. A buyer can estimate shipping cost or delivery time to the U.S.

  6. Your MOQ, lead time and sample policy are on the site.

  7. Certifications, test reports or quality steps are shown.

  8. Your quote form works and you received a test message this month.

  9. Every new inquiry gets a reply within one U.S. business day.

  10. Your company shows up when you search Google for what you make plus "supplier".

  11. AI assistants (ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity) can describe your company correctly.

  12. Inquiries go into one list or CRM automatically, not copied by hand.
