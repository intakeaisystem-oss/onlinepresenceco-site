What the audit checks

Five groups of checks, in plain words:

  • Your call button.

    Is there a call or quote button on the first phone screen, and a tap-to-call link?

  • Your form.

    Is there a form, and is it set up to send somewhere? Nathan sends a test by hand before you get your results.

  • Your speed.

    How heavy your page is and how fast it answers. This is a rough reading from our side, not a phone test.

  • Search basics.

    Page title, description, one main heading, https and a sitemap. Business details search engines can read, and one phone number used the same way across the page.

  • AI search readiness.

    Are AI search tools allowed to read your site? Is there structured data, question-and-answer content, and a summary file written for AI tools?

Some things need a person. Nathan tests your form, looks at your first screen on a real phone and checks your Google Business Profile.

Questions owners ask

What's the catch with the free audit?

Fair question. It's how Nathan meets owners. There's no cost and no card. You choose: Nathan emails you the fix list, or walks you through it on a call at the time you picked. The fix list is yours to keep. If you say no, that's the end of it. No texts, no spam.

How much does it cost?

Every business is different. Nathan gives you a straight quote after a short call. The quote is built from what your audit finds and the work you want more of, so you only pay for what you need. If it's more than you want to spend, he can start you smaller, like fewer pages first.

Is my website mine, and is my domain in my name?

Yes. Your domain is in your name from day one, and the site is yours once the setup is paid.

Can you get me to the top of Google?

No. We never promise rankings. Nobody honestly can. Be careful of anyone who says they can. We fix what's holding your site back and keep your business details right. Every month we show you what we did and what we checked.

My nephew (or a web company) already does my site. Why would I need you?

Maybe you don't. We're not here to knock anyone's work. The only question is whether the site does its job: can a customer on a phone call you, and does the form reach you? The audit checks the first, Nathan tests the form by hand, and you can hand the fix list straight to whoever runs your site. One thing worth knowing either way: whose name is on your domain, and who has the login.

You get a plain written agreement to read before you pay anything. It says what's included, how billing works and how to cancel. Nathan goes over it with you on the call.
