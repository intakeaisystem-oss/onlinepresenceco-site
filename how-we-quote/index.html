How we quote

No packages. No price list. A quote built from what your audit finds.

You pay for the fixes your business needs, not for a bundle someone else picked. Here's how we get from your website to a straight number.

Get my free auditCall Nathan: (630) 765-0559
  1. 1

    The audit

    The free audit checks your call button, your form, your speed, your search basics and whether AI search tools can read your site. Nathan runs a quick version before your call. Nathan also runs the full check. He looks at what only a person can: your form, your first phone screen and your Google Business Profile.

    The full list of checks

  2. 2

    The findings

    Every finding comes with a plain fix. Each fix points to one of the five things we do, so you can see why it's on the list.

    What the audit findsWhat it points to
    No call button on the first phone screen, or a form with nowhere to sendWebsites
    Missing page titles or no business details search engines can readLocal SEO
    AI search tools blocked, or no question-and-answer contentAI search (GEO)
    No visitor analytics, so nobody can see where people leaveVisitor Insights
    Slow or heavy pages, or no secure https addressHosting + Care
  3. 3

    Your plan

    On the call, Nathan asks what work you want more of and where it comes from today. He keeps the fixes that matter for that, and drops the rest. If a fix is something you or your web person can do, he says so.

  4. 4

    A straight quote

    He tells you the price once, in plain words. No range, no "around", and no package to talk you into. Then you get a plain written agreement to read before you pay anything.

What sets the price

  • How many services and towns need their own page
  • A new site or a rebuild of the one you have
  • Whether you want local SEO and AI search work now, later or not at all
  • Whether you want Visitor Insights
  • How often you expect small edits

If it's more than you want to spend

Say so. Nathan won't knock the price down for nothing. He can shrink the plan instead, like fewer pages to start or search work later, and quote it again on the spot.
