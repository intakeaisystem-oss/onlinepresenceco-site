With no shop to drive past, the search is your storefront

When someone nearby types “detailing near me” or “car detailing” plus their town, Google usually shows a map with a few businesses first, then websites. Small gaps there decide who gets the booking, and you never hear about the customers who picked someone else.

No service area set

A detailer who goes to the customer can hide the address and list the towns served instead. If that is blank or wrong, Google has less to match you to a nearby search.

Photos that look old

People judge a detail by the photos. A profile with nothing recent looks quiet, even when you are booked solid.

Booking takes a hunt

If a customer on a phone can't call, text or book from your listing or site in one tap, the next name is one tap away.

Everything lives on Facebook

A Facebook page helps people who already follow you. It is often not what a stranger sees when they search.
