02Site structure

Pages a supplier website usually includes

The exact pages depend on your business. A warehouse, a distributor and a factory need different pages.

  1. HomeWho you are, what you do, where
  2. About UsCompany background, factory or warehouse, team
  3. ProductsBy category, with photos and specifications
  4. CapabilitiesEquipment, processes, capacity, warehousing
  5. QualityInspection, and certifications you actually hold
  6. Contact / RFQWhat a quote needs and how to reach you
