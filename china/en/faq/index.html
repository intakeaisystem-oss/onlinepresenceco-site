Frequently asked questions

If your question isn't here, contact Nathan directly.

Contact Nathan
What language do we communicate in?

English. Online Presence Co. is run by Nathan alone. He works in English, with no Chinese-language support or professional translation. Many businesses use email and translation tools.

What language is the website in?

English. It's written for American buyers. The Chinese pages you see here explain our service to you.

What information do I need to provide?

A company overview, product information and photos, manufacturing or warehouse capabilities if relevant, certifications you actually hold, and contact details. Nathan tells you exactly what's needed in the first conversation.

I only have materials in Chinese. Is that OK?

Tell Nathan in the first conversation and he'll tell you whether he can work with it. We don't offer professional translation. All English content goes to you for review before launch.

Who owns the domain?

You do. Your domain is in your name from day one, and the site is yours once the setup is paid.

Who hosts the website?

We do, including the security certificate (https), backups and uptime checks.

What does monthly management include?

Hosting, maintenance and content updates, such as changes to products, company details or contact information. The details are made clear before we start.

How do I change the website later?

Contact Nathan and tell him what to change.

How much does it cost?

Every business is different. After a short conversation, Nathan gives you a short written plan and one clear quote.

Will the website guarantee inquiries or orders?

No. A website makes it easier for American buyers to learn about you and contact you, but inquiries, orders, rankings and buyer trust depend on your products, pricing, service, the market and many other factors. We don't promise them.

Is this an online store for U.S. consumers?

No. We build company websites that help American business buyers learn about your company and contact you.

Do you have an office in China?

No. Online Presence Co. is based in Illinois and run by Nathan.

How do I contact Nathan?

See the contact page. Nathan works on U.S. Central Time, 13 to 14 hours behind Beijing time.

Want American buyers to understand your company more easily?

Start with a conversation with Nathan about your company, your products and the buyers you want to reach. If it isn't a fit, he'll tell you.
