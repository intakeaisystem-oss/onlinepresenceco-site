02Writing in English

Before and after: more specific

These examples are made up to show the writing. A real site is written from the information you provide.

Common wording

We are professional manufacturer with high quality and best price, welcome to inquiry!

→

Clearer wording

We machine aluminum and steel parts to your drawings. Send us your drawing and quantity, and our sales team will reply with a quote.

WhySaying what you make and what the buyer should do next is more useful than 'professional', 'high quality' or 'best price'.

Common wording

Our factory have advanced equipments and strict QC system.

→

Clearer wording

Our factory runs CNC machining and in-house inspection. Every batch is checked against your drawing before it ships.

WhyName the equipment and the inspection instead of 'advanced' and 'strict'. Correct grammar also reassures buyers.

Common wording

Product: Model XJ-2034A

→

Clearer wording

Aluminum mounting bracket. 6061 aluminum, anodized. Sizes from 50 to 300 mm. Used in solar racking and machinery frames.

WhyA model number alone tells the buyer nothing. Give the name, material, sizes and uses.

03What buyers check

What buyers usually look for on a supplier website

  1. 01

    Who you are

    Company name, location and main business.

  2. 02

    What you make

    Product categories, photos, specifications and uses.

  3. 03

    What you can do

    Equipment, processes, capacity or warehousing.

  4. 04

    How you control quality

    Inspection steps, and certifications you actually hold.

  5. 05

    How to request a quote

    What to send and who it goes to.

  6. 06

    Whether the site is kept up

    Current information and contact details that work.
