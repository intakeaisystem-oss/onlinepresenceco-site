    02Glossary

    U.S. buyer terms that are easy to misread

    Short explanations of common usage. For actual trade and payment terms, your contract governs.

    RFQRequest for Quotation
    A buyer's request for your price, usually with drawings, quantities and requirements.
    Lead time
    The time from order confirmation to delivery or shipment.
    FOBFree On Board
    The seller loads the goods onto the ship at the named port. Freight and risk after that are the buyer's. Incoterms FOB is for sea freight; 'FOB' in U.S. domestic contracts can mean something else.
    EXWEx Works
    The buyer collects the goods at the seller's factory or warehouse and carries nearly all costs and risk from there.
    DDPDelivered Duty Paid
    The seller delivers to the buyer's named place and handles U.S. import clearance and duties.
    Spec sheetSpecification sheet
    A document listing a product's materials, dimensions and performance.
    Datasheet
    Like a spec sheet, common for electronic and industrial products.
    Drawing / CAD file
    The design drawing a buyer supplies.
    Net 30
    Payment due within 30 days of the invoice.
    HTS codeHarmonized Tariff Schedule code
    The U.S. tariff classification code that sets the import duty for a product.
    Distributor
    A company that buys from manufacturers and resells to its own customers.
