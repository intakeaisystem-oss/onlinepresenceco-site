01What to prepare

What to prepare

English materials work best. If you only have Chinese materials, tell Nathan in the first conversation.

Your domain is in your name from day one, and the site is yours once the setup is paid.

  • Company overview: when you started, your main business, where your factory or warehouse is
  • Product information: categories, specifications, uses and product photos
  • Manufacturing, distribution or warehouse capabilities (if relevant)
  • Certifications you actually hold (only real ones)
  • How buyers should contact you: email, phone and so on
  • If you already have a domain, access to your domain account
