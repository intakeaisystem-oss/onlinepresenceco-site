Questions owners ask before they hire anyone

Ten of them, answered plainly.

All questions

I paid a company every month and the phone never rang. Why would you be different?

No one can promise your phone rings, and we won't. We build your site so a customer on a phone can call you in one tap, and every month we show you what we did and what we checked.

Why should I trust someone new?

You'd be one of Nathan's first clients, so there are no reviews to show yet. What you can check: your own free audit, the sample sites on this page, the terms in writing before you pay, and that your domain is in your name from day one.

How much does it cost?

Every business is different. Nathan gives you a straight quote after a short call. One price in plain words, and the scope in writing before you pay anything. How quoting works.

Can you guarantee rankings or more calls?

No. We never promise rankings. Nobody honestly can. What we do: make sure the people who find you can see what you do and reach you in one tap, and, if you keep the care plan, show you every month what we did.

Will I be stuck in a contract?

You get a plain written agreement to read before you pay anything. It says what's included, how billing works and how to cancel. Nathan goes over it with you on the call.

Who owns the site if I leave?

You do. Your domain is in your name from day one, and the site is yours once the setup is paid. If you leave, you get a copy of your site files and help moving your domain.

What does the free audit include?

A check of your call button, your form, your speed and your search setup, plus Nathan's own look. You get a plain fix list, by email or walked through on a call, your choice. It's yours to keep, hire us or not. See a sample audit.

I get all my work from referrals. Do I even need a website?

If you're booked out on referrals and happy, maybe not. A website doesn't replace word of mouth. It gives the people your customers send somewhere to land when they look you up before calling.

How much of my time does this take?

Mostly one call, then your photos and a few answers. Nathan asks for phone photos of your real jobs, your services and the towns you work in. Nothing goes live without your yes.

What happens after it's live?

Hosting, backups, updates and small edits, plus a monthly report, if you keep the care plan. Same person, same number: Nathan.
