The gaps

Small problems that cost big U.S. orders

Often the product is fine. What loses the order is something small on the way from "interested" to "quote request sent".

Shipping shown the wrong way

Sizes in cm and kg only, no idea of the billable weight, Incoterms with no explanation, no delivery time to the U.S. American buyers can't estimate the real cost, so they don't ask.

English that reads like a translation

"We are professional manufacturer with best quality" tells a buyer nothing. Clear, specific English about what you make and what to send you does.

Quote requests that go nowhere

Broken forms, emails that land in spam, replies two days later because of the time zone. The buyer has already written to three other suppliers.

Invisible on Google and AI search

U.S. buyers search Google and now ask ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity for suppliers. If your site doesn't answer their questions clearly, you aren't in the answer.

Only a marketplace store

An independent company website gives buyers another place to check you before they trust a marketplace listing.

Missing the facts buyers need

MOQ, lead time, sample policy, certifications, materials, tolerances, payment terms. If they aren't on the page, the buyer has to guess.

A slow or broken site

Pages that load slowly from the U.S., no secure HTTPS lock, or a layout that breaks on a phone.

Hours of manual work

Copying inquiries into spreadsheets, retyping the same quote, forgetting follow-ups. Small automations can take that work off your team.

Frequently asked questions

Who do you work with?

Manufacturers, factories, exporters, wholesalers, distributors and brands outside the United States that sell, or want to sell, to American businesses or consumers, in any country, for example China, Canada, Mexico, Vietnam, India, Korea or Europe.

Do I need to speak English?

No. Write to us in your language. We reply in English and in your language using translation tools. The website we build for your buyers is in English.

How much does it cost?

We don't have a price list, because every job is different. A single fix costs much less than a full website. After a short conversation we send a written quote before you pay anything.

Can you fix just one thing?

Yes. You can start with one fix, such as a working quote form, a shipping calculator or clear English product pages, then decide what's next.

I already sell on Alibaba, Amazon or another marketplace. Do I still need a website?

An independent company website gives buyers another place to check you before they trust a supplier. Your site can link to your marketplace store, and it is something you control and own.

What is GEO?

Generative engine optimization: making your site easy for AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews to understand and describe correctly. It uses clear answers, structured data and consistent facts. No one can guarantee an AI will mention you; clear, consistent facts make it easier for AI tools to describe you correctly.
