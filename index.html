How much does it cost?

Every business is different. Nathan gives you a straight quote after a short call. The quote is built from what your audit finds and the work you want more of, so you only pay for what you need. If it's more than you want to spend, he can start you smaller, like fewer pages first.

Yes. Your domain is in your name from day one, and the site is yours once the setup is paid.
