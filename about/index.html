About Nathan

Get started todayCall Nathan: (630) 765-0559

Nathan works construction in McHenry County. He carries the lumber, he's on the job site, and he knows how work comes in for people in the trades: a phone call, a referral, someone looking you up before they call.

He started Online Presence Co. to handle the part most owners don't have time for: a website people can find, that works on a phone, and that gets the call or the quote request to you.

He answers his own phone. If he's on a job, he calls you back. When you fill in a form here, he's the one who calls you, at the time you picked. You won't get handed off to a rep after you pay.

How he works

  • He looks at your site first, and tells you if it's already fine.
  • Every business is different. Nathan gives you a straight quote after a short call.
  • Your domain is in your name from day one, and the site is yours once the setup is paid.
  • Nothing goes live without your yes.
  • We never promise rankings. Nobody honestly can.

Where things stand

You'd be one of Nathan's first clients, so there are no reviews to show you yet. What he can show you is your own audit and exactly what he'd change.

He uses AI tools to build and check sites faster, and every page goes through a script that checks for made-up facts and broken links before you see it. You still deal with Nathan.
