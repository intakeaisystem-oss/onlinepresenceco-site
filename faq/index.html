Price and terms

How much does a website cost for a small business?

Every business is different. Nathan gives you a straight quote after a short call. It's built from what your audit finds and the work you want more of, so you pay only for what you need. If that's more than you want to spend, he can start smaller, like fewer pages first.

Why don't you have packages?

A package makes you pay for things you don't need and can skip things you do. A roofer with a slow site and a cleaner with no site need different work. Your audit shows what's missing, and the quote covers that.

Do I have to sign a long contract?

You get a plain written agreement to read before you pay anything. It says what's included, how billing works and how to cancel. Nathan goes over it with you on the call.

Is my website mine, and is my domain in my name?

Yes. Your domain is in your name from day one, and the site is yours once the setup is paid.

The free audit

What's the catch with the free audit?

It's how Nathan meets owners. It costs nothing and needs no card. You choose: Nathan emails you the fix list, or walks you through it on a call at the time you picked. The fix list is yours to keep. If you say no, that's the end of it. We don't text, and we don't spam.

What does the free audit check?

Your call button on a phone screen, your tap-to-call link, whether your form is set up to send somewhere, your page speed, your search basics (title, description, headings, https, sitemap, business details) and whether AI search tools can read your site. By hand, Nathan also checks that your form really reaches you, how your first phone screen looks and your Google Business Profile.

Search, Google and AI search

Do I need a website if I have a Google Business Profile?

Not always. Your Google Business Profile shows your phone, hours, photos and reviews on Google Search and Maps. A website is the page you control: your services, the towns you cover, your own photos and a quote form that reaches you. If your profile already brings the work you want, Nathan will tell you on the call. We can set up and look after both.

Can you help with my Google Business Profile?

Yes. We set it up or clean it up, keep your services, hours and photos current, make your name, address and phone match your website, and give you a simple way to ask every customer for a review. We never write reviews you didn't get. We never promise rankings. Nobody honestly can.

What's the difference between a website and local SEO?

Your website is what customers see when they find you. Local SEO is the ongoing work that helps people nearby find you in the first place: your Google Business Profile kept current, your services and towns spelled out, and your name, address and phone the same everywhere.

Can you get me to the top of Google?

No. We never promise rankings. Nobody honestly can. Be careful of anyone who says they can. We fix what's holding your site back and keep your business details right. Every month we show you what we did and checked.

What is AI search, and can you get ChatGPT to recommend me?

AI search is when someone asks ChatGPT, Google's AI Overviews or Perplexity a question, like "who fixes furnaces near me?", and gets an answer instead of a list of links. We set your site up so those tools can read it: clear business details, structured data, plain question-and-answer content, a short summary file for AI tools, and settings that let their search crawlers in. Each month we ask them your customers' questions and show you what they said. We report what the AI tools say. We never promise placement.

Your visitors and your privacy

How do you find where visitors leave my site?

With Visitor Insights. Standard analytics tools show the pages people leave from, how far they scroll, where they tap and nothing happens, and which form field they quit on. Each month you get a Leak Report: the top 3 places visitors leave, the likely reason with the proof, and the fix. These tools are listed in your site's privacy notice.

Do you track me on this site?

Only in a small, private way. We count visits with our own simple counter. It uses no cookies and no ad pixels, and the data goes to the same private Google Sheet as our forms, nowhere else. The counter doesn't keep your IP address or anything you type into a form before you send it. It does note which form field someone stops at, so we can fix our own forms. If your browser sends a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal, we don't count you at all. Details are in our Privacy Notice.

Working together

Do you only work with contractors?

No. We work with contractors and trades, restaurants and cafes, salons and barbers, auto shops, cleaners, retail shops and any other local business. In person around Crystal Lake, McHenry County and the Chicago suburbs. Online, for businesses anywhere in the US.

How long does it take?

It depends on how many pages you need and how soon we get your photos and details. Nathan puts a target date in your written scope before you pay anything. If your photos come later, the date moves with them.

What do you need from me?

Photos of your work (phone photos are fine), your services, the towns you cover, and a short call to start. If you have a site, we'll need access to your domain. If you don't, Nathan helps you register one in your name. After that, we look after it.

My nephew (or a web company) already does my site. Why would I need you?

Maybe you don't. The only question is whether the site does its job: can a customer on a phone call you, and does the form reach you? The audit checks the first, Nathan tests the form by hand, and you can give the fix list to whoever runs your site. Either way, know whose name is on your domain and who has the login.

I get all my work from referrals. Do I even need a website?

If you're booked out on referrals and happy, maybe not. A website doesn't replace word of mouth. It gives the people your customers send somewhere to land when they look you up before calling.

Can I build my own website with a site builder instead?

Yes, you can. What matters is whether a customer on a phone can call you in one tap and whether your form actually reaches you. The free audit checks that on any site, whoever built it, and the fix list is yours to keep.

Have you done this before?

You'd be one of Nathan's first clients, so there are no reviews to show you yet. What he can show you is your own audit and exactly what he'd change. AI tools help us build and check faster. You still deal with Nathan, and nothing goes live without your yes.
