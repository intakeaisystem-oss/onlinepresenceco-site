Top 5 fixes, in plain English (ranked by how much they affect leads)

  1. Put a "Call now" and a "Get a quote" button at the very top of the page, visible on a phone without scrolling. → Covered by: Websites
  2. Connect the form so every enquiry lands in an inbox and a call list, then send a test. → Covered by: Websites
  3. Add LocalBusiness schema (name, phone, address or service area, hours) so Google and AI tools read the basics. → Covered by: Local SEO (it also counts toward AI search visibility)
  4. Add a short FAQ that answers the questions buyers ask (price range, area served, how long it takes). → Covered by: AI search visibility (GEO)
  5. Add a "book a time" link so people who won't call can still book. → Covered by: Hosting + Care (small edit)

Asked on the call (an outside check can't see these)

  1. Do missed calls get an automatic reply? We can't test that from outside.
  2. Does the form actually deliver? Only a test submission proves it.
  3. Do the Business Profile and directory listings use the same name and phone as the site? We check those by hand.

The report never claims lost revenue or lost leads. Every number in it was measured on the run.

How the audit becomes a custom plan (SAMPLE)

On the call, Nathan goes over the report with the owner and asks what matters to him. In this made-up example the owner says:

  1. He wants more storm-repair and roof-replacement calls in Anytown and two nearby towns.
  2. Most of his work comes from referrals, and people "look him up" before they call.
  3. He answers his own phone and doesn't want a chat widget.

So the plan keeps only the findings that serve those goals.
