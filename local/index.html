Most Businesses Lose Customers Before the First Call.

People search, compare and decide within minutes. These are the five places a good business drops out.

  • A weak or outdated websiteSlow pages, old photos and small text on a phone make a good business look closed.
  • Poor online visibilityYou don't appear on Google, Maps or in AI answers when someone nearby searches for what you do.
  • Too few qualified leadsVisitors arrive, find no clear way to call, text or book, and leave.
  • Marketing that doesn't pay backTime and money go into posts and ads that send people to a page that doesn't convert.
  • Competitors who look strongerThe business with current reviews, real photos and a faster site gets the call, even when your work is better.

Questions Owners Ask

All questions
What kinds of businesses do you work with?

Any business customers look up before they buy: trades, mobile services like detailing, salons, restaurants, clinics, shops, offices and online stores. The audit shows what your business needs.

Do I need a whole new website?

Not always. Sometimes fixing your Google profile and a few pages does more than a rebuild. The audit tells you which.

I get all my work from word of mouth. Why bother?

Even people who were referred to you look you up before they call. If they find nothing, an old site or a competitor first, some of those referrals never reach you.

Can you get me to #1 on Google?

No one can honestly promise a ranking. What we do is fix the things that make it hard for Google, Maps and AI tools to show you, and keep improving them.

What does it cost?

Every business needs something different, so there is no one-size price. After your audit you get a clear written quote before any work starts.
