Small things that send customers to the other guy

Your work is good. Small things online lose the call.

Not on the map

Your Google Business Profile is missing, unclaimed or half filled in, so you don't show up when someone nearby searches for what you do.

A site that breaks on a phone

Most local searches happen on a phone. Tiny text, slow pages or a menu that doesn't open sends people back to the results.

No easy way to reach you

No tap-to-call button, a form that goes nowhere, or a phone number buried at the bottom of the page.

Old hours, old photos, few reviews

Customers read that as "maybe closed." A current profile with recent photos and reviews reads as busy and trusted.

Nothing about where you work

If your site never names your towns and services, Google has no reason to show you for them.

Invisible to AI search

People now ask ChatGPT and Google's AI for "a good plumber near me." Clear, consistent facts about you make it easier for those tools to describe you.

Do I need a whole new website?

Not always. Sometimes fixing your Google profile and a few pages does more than a rebuild. The audit tells you which.

I get all my work from word of mouth. Why bother?

Even people who were referred to you look you up before they call. If they find nothing, an old site or a competitor first, some of those referrals never reach you.
